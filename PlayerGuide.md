# Cosmic Ascendancy - Player Guide

Welcome to the endgame! Once your empire has reached a massive scale, you are ready to construct your **Ascendant Capital**.

## 1. Founding Your Capital
To begin, you must found an **Ascendancy Beacon**. You can build this station just like any other factory or mine.
Once built, interact with the station and click "Manage Beacon" to open the Ascendancy UI.

**Activation:**
Activating the beacon forces its sector to stay loaded forever. You will begin paying an Upkeep cost every **45 Minutes**. If you fail to pay the upkeep, the beacon will lose power and shut down, meaning the sector will unload.
You can only have up to **3 Active Beacons** simultaneously!

## 2. Upgrading Tiers
Your beacon starts at Tier 1. By sinking enormous amounts of Credits and Ores (scaling up to Ogonite and Avorion) into the beacon, you can upgrade it to Tier 5.
As you upgrade your tier, **all ships in your entire empire** will receive permanent global multipliers to their Damage, Hull, and Shields!

## 3. The Grand Toll
Because your sector is always loaded, NPC traders will constantly jump in. Your beacon acts as an intergalactic border checkpoint! Every AI ship that jumps into your sector will automatically pay a toll directly into your vault.
*   **Wartime Premium**: If an AI ship belongs to a faction currently fighting a massive war, they will pay up to **+50% extra toll** to seek shelter in your sector!

## 4. Defending Against Sieges
Your capital generates immense wealth and power, which naturally attracts danger. Every **3 to 6 hours** of playtime, a gargantuan invasion fleet will jump directly into your sector to destroy the beacon!
*   **Warning**: If the beacon is destroyed, you will instantly lose all of your global Ascendant buffs!
*   **Advice**: You must build heavily fortified Defense Stations and leave heavily armed defense fleets patrolling the sector 24/7.
*   **Rewards**: Defeating the siege drops a massive explosion of Legendary and Exotic weapons!

## 5. The Stellar Forge
The ultimate resource sink. Interact with your beacon and select "Stellar Forge". Here, you can exchange **Billions of Credits** to asynchronously craft a custom, God-Tier weapon.
The forge runs in real-time, taking **24 Hours** to complete.

**Wartime Innovation (Pro Tip):**
The weapons crafted at the forge receive insane damage multipliers. These multipliers scale based on two factors:
1.  **Core Proximity**: Forging weapons at the Galactic Core yields a +200% damage bonus!
2.  **War Heat**: Forging a weapon while your empire is currently fighting a massive, intense war yields up to an additional +150% damage bonus! 
By combining these, you can forge an apocalyptic super-weapon!
